01 / ABOUT EDU-E-LITE

Strong foundations.
Clear thinking.

Edu-e-Lite is a focused tutorial and academic learning centre for students pursuing Computer Science and related undergraduate and postgraduate programmes.

We support B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering, BCA and MCA students through concept-oriented teaching across Engineering Mathematics, core Computer Science subjects and programming. Our aim is to make challenging topics easier to understand through clear explanations, structured practice and problem-solving.
